# 03 · Organization


The organization is centralized: the **CEO directly runs data, product planning, and media**, while three departments handle development, content, and marketing. The source document defines roles by **department** (no individual names are listed in it).


## CEO — direct responsibilities

- **Overall leadership & decision making**
- **Data accumulation**
  - Research and collect raw data on piracy damage (individual revenue loss, industry decline, etc.)
  - Record data sources
  - Analyze collected data into graphs/charts
  - Hand analyzed data to the Development department
- **Website product management**
  - Design the initial prototype (website workflow + visual design) and hand it to Development
  - Give feedback after the first prototype, and at every refinement (focus on the major items: overall design, features to add)
- **Media content management**
  - Conduct creator interviews based on the Content department's plan (reach out + interview)
  - Give feedback on the Content department's plans/prototypes
- **Others** — website translation (Korean, English, Japanese)


## Development Department

- **Prototype development (pre-launch)** — build the first prototype design from the data, workflow, and visual design; refine from feedback
- **Website publication** — publish the finished prototype design
- **Website refinement (post-launch)** — refine from feedback, fix errors, improve design/visuals
- **Website analytics & tracking** — track key metrics (users, time-on-page, etc.)


## Contents Production Department

- **Create media contents** (including interviews)
  - **Primary goal:** raise awareness through informative/entertaining content about the issue and the webtoon industry (e.g. short videos, social posts)
  - **Secondary goal:** drive traffic to the website via links embedded in that content
- Produce first-draft (prototype) content, refine from feedback, and upload


## Marketing Department

- **Create social media posts** — advertise the website itself across platforms. These are **purely promotional** and are distinct from the Content department's media contents.
- **Create partnerships**
  - With webtoon platforms / other organizations — for promotion and further use
  - With schools — to promote the site to students


## Two kinds of content — a key distinction

| | Made by | Purpose |
|---|---------|---------|
| **Media contents** | Contents Production Dept. | Awareness (primary) + site traffic (secondary) |
| **Social media posts** | Marketing Dept. | Site promotion only |


## Decision flow

Centralized around the CEO, who approves every department's output:

```
            ┌──────────── CEO ────────────┐
            │ (data · product · media · approval)
            ▼            ▼            ▼
       Development   Contents      Marketing
        (build)     (awareness)   (promotion + partnerships)
            ▲            │
            └── data ────┘   interview plans → CEO runs interviews
```
