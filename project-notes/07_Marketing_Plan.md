# 07 · Marketing Plan


Marketing is defined in two blocks: **(A)** promoting the website itself, and **(B)** the viral media-content strategy. Each defines its own audiences, value props, and channels. The "Marketing Report" holds the substance; the "Marketing Plan" doc is currently an empty skeleton.


## Block A — the website itself


### Target audience

| Segment | Definition |
|---------|-----------|
| Webtoon Explorers | Fans looking for fun series and official platforms |
| Information Seekers | People wanting well-organized info on a specific series (esp. hardcore fans) |
| Convenience Seeker | Users who use piracy sites only because everything is in one place |
| International Fans | Fans outside Korea seeking official translations in their language |
| Safety-Conscious Readers | Readers who want to keep their device safe from threats |
| Other Huge Webtoon Fans | Other enthusiastic fans |


### Value propositions

- **Webtoon Discovery Hub** — explore via rankings & recommendations
  - **Cross-platform ranking system** spanning the whole industry (shows real industry-wide trends)
  - Original recommendation categories instead of plain genres — e.g. *"Webtoons that feel like a rainy Sunday"*, *"Protagonists with actual brain cells"*
  - **Login-based personalized recommendations** + user **reading lists**
- **Information Compilation** — consolidated descriptions, genres, ratings, reviews
- **Comprehensive Platform Search** — official platforms carrying a series (with cost, language/region, latest-chapter info)
- **Dangers of Using Piracy Sites** — security threats, how to check device safety
- **Behind the Scenes of Webtoon Creation** — creator interviews (schedules, staff, process)


### Channels

| Channel | Goal | Pros | Cons |
|---------|------|------|------|
| Short-form video (Reels, TikTok, YT Shorts) | Go viral with fun content + promote the site | Mass reach to Gen Z/Alpha webtoon audience | Unpredictable algorithm; needs constant content |
| Internet communities (DCinside, Naver Cafe, Reddit, Discord) | Build trust; become a community tool | Direct access to hardcore fans | They dislike self-promotion — must be an active member first |
| SEO | Visibility/brand discovery; **intercept** piracy users searching for a series | High-intent traffic | Competitive keywords |
| Collaborations (creators, platforms, influencers) | Build trust/authority | Instant authority & credibility | Hard to reach / get permission |
| QR-code guerrilla marketing | Catch enthusiasts at webtoon events | Precise targeting, face-to-face | Labor-intensive; needs physical materials |


## Block B — viral media content


### Target audience (distinct from Block A)

| Segment | Definition |
|---------|-----------|
| Unaware Fans | Don't realize piracy harms creators and themselves |
| Budget-Constrained Fans | Can't afford official platforms (esp. teens) |
| Skeptic Fans | Think webtoon companies/creators already make enough money |
| Those who just don't care | — |


### Core messages

- **The Personal Stake** — the risk piracy sites pose to the user
- **Creator Connection** — empathy via creator interviews & process
- **Webtoon Industry Collapse** — why quality is dropping; the market could disappear
- **Quality Gap** — official vs piracy translation/image quality
- **Entertaining Topics first** — lead with fun content; link the site secondarily (end of content, comments)


### Channels (detailed)

- **Short-form:** YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels
- **Community engagement:**
  - Korea: Naver Cafe, DCinside, TheQoo, ArcaLive, FMKorea, Inven
  - Non-Korea: Reddit, Discord, Threads, X (Twitter)
- **Collaborations:** platform partnerships / creators / **anti-piracy groups** (e.g. Alliance for Creativity and Entertainment)
- **Pinterest** (as a visual search engine)
- **SEO**
- **Wikis** — add a small helpful note + site link in "external links" sections
- **Offline marketing** — *not done yet*


### Entertaining content topics

- Webtoon recommendations
- Behind the scenes of webtoon creation (creator schedules, publishing process)
- Specific webtoon analysis (e.g. *"hidden details in chapter 100 of [series]"*)
- Ways to earn cookies (current events, sales, free-cookie methods)
