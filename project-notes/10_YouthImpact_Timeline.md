# 10 · YouthImpact Timeline (Go-Forward Plan)


A forward roadmap prepared by the consulting firm **YouthImpact** (doc "최지호_타임라인", owner song@youthimpact.com, last updated 2026-06-23). This is more recent and more concrete than the original 2025 roadmap in [02 · Project Plan & Timeline](./02_Project_Plan_Timeline.md), and centers everything on the **price-comparison feature** as the wedge.

**Total duration: ~8 months across 4 phases.**


## Phase 1 — Data collection + website development (1 month)


### Data collection

- First pass: collect pricing data for the **top 20 popular titles each** from **4 platforms** — Naver Webtoon, KakaoPage, Ridi, Series — for **80 titles total**.
- Per title, organize by item: list price per chapter, discounted price, range of free chapters, ongoing events (first chapter free, wait-for-free, etc.).
- Continuously add more titles per platform to enrich the database, expanding to a cumulative **~160 titles**.


### Website development

- Design the wireframe and UI flow for the price-comparison feature based on the collected data.
- Connect the pricing database to the Fairtoon platform; build the feature that pulls per-platform prices when a title is searched.


## Phase 2 — Data collection + website launch + beta user test (1 month)


### Beta test

- Test the Phase-1 price-comparison feature and improved UI with a beta group (friends, acquaintances, school clubs — ~**15–20 people**).
- Have participants search titles via the price-comparison feature and complete a short Google Form survey rating accuracy and ease of use.
- Compile errors found during testing (missing price info, search-result errors) and usability improvements.
- Apply high-priority feedback first; keep enriching the database and updating new titles and price changes.


### Website launch

- Officially launch the Fairtoon website including the price-comparison feature.
- Produce and distribute launch-announcement content (SNS card-news, infographics, launch posts).


## Phase 3 — Marketing & events (3 months)

Use the price-comparison launch as a hook to expand new-user inflow and raise Fairtoon awareness.


### Events

- **"Find My Cheapest Webtoon" challenge** — search a title via the feature and post the cheapest platform to SNS.
- **Piracy-awareness quiz event** — solve quizzes on copyright and creator harm.
- **Legal-webtoon proof review event** — post a legal-platform payment screenshot and a review.
- **Creator support-message campaign** — users leave support messages for creators, delivered later during interviews.


### Marketing

- **Instagram:** harm-statistics card-news, creator-interview Reels, how-to-use content, event promotion.
- **YouTube / TikTok:** piracy-risk shorts, full creator interviews, feature demo videos.
- **Blog / Naver Cafe:** informational posts ("how to read webtoons legally and cheaply") to drive search traffic.
- **Online communities (Everytime, webtoon gallery, etc.):** event promotion and informational posts.
- **Collaborations:** content collabs with copyright groups, creator associations, and webtoon influencers.


## Phase 4 — Interviews + creator-related activities (3 months)

Building on the traffic and awareness gained from events, pursue the original purpose — **creator protection** — in earnest.


### Webtoon market pricing & revenue-structure analysis

- Research per-platform creator settlement ratios, revenue-share structures, and the **MG (Minimum Guarantee)** system at Naver Webtoon, KakaoPage, etc.; analyze how legal-platform use affects creator income.
- Write a pricing-policy / revenue-structure comparison report from the analysis.


### Creator interviews

- Re-review the existing creator database to prioritize creators who actively speak out on copyright / piracy issues.
- Send cooperation-request emails to webtoon associations, copyright bodies, and creator-rights groups.
- Conduct interviews, record on video/audio, and compile key statements and statistically meaningful harm cases.
- Edit interview content, post it in the Fairtoon "Creator Interviews" section, and distribute across SNS / marketing channels.
