# 01 · Project Overview


## What FairToon is

FairToon is a website that **collects, analyzes, and visualizes the damage caused by webtoon piracy**, and steers readers toward legal platforms in order to reduce the use of illegal piracy sites.

It began in January 2025 as an independent project by Jiho Choi, a high-school student (sophomore at Cheongna Dalton International School), conceived in part as a portfolio project for college applications. It runs with **no marketing budget**, which shapes much of the strategy (original content + SEO instead of paid acquisition).


## Mission & positioning

The source material does not contain a single formal mission statement, but the intent is consistent across documents:

> Reduce the use of illegal webtoon piracy sites by raising public awareness — and by making the **legal** path so convenient that piracy loses its main advantage.


## Core strategic choice — self-interest, not moralizing

The defining strategic decision is **how** the message is framed:

- **Not** moral lecturing ("piracy hurts creators, so stop").
- **Instead** self-interest framing ("piracy sites are dangerous to *you* and *your device*"), plus making the legal alternative genuinely easier to use.

This is why the site pairs a **practical utility** (find where to legally read a series) with **awareness content** (why piracy is risky), rather than being a pure activism campaign.


## Target users (two layers)

| Layer | Who | How they arrive |
|-------|-----|-----------------|
| Primary | Readers who already want to read legally | Use the **Search** feature directly |
| Secondary | Readers unaware the site exists | Arrive via **SEO / Articles** (e.g. searching "is it safe to use webtoon piracy sites" → lands on an article → discovers the site) |


## Goals

1. Build a useful, wiki-like **legal-platform finder** for webtoon series.
2. Earn organic discovery through **original, SEO-friendly content** (Articles).
3. Reduce piracy use by lowering the friction of reading legally **and** raising awareness of piracy's personal risks.
4. Use **creator interviews** and **viral media content** to build credibility and reach.


## How the pieces fit together

```
        Awareness / reach                 Utility / retention
   ┌───────────────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────────────┐
   Viral media content (Shorts) ──►   Search (legal-platform finder)
   Articles (SEO)            ──────►   Detail pages (wiki info)
   Creator interviews        ──────►   Stay Safe (piracy risks)
   Marketing / partnerships  ──────►   About
   └───────────────────────────┘     └──────────────────────────┘
```

Media content and marketing **bring people in**; the Search experience and Stay Safe content **convert and keep them on the legal path**.
