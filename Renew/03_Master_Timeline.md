## Phase 1 — July: Build (website production complete)

**Goal:** ship a production-ready site by Jul 31.

- **Website production** — take the preview to a feature-complete production site: (1) discovery (find/recommend), (2) **legal-site aggregator** — on each title's detail page, "Read on: [Naver][Kakao]…" buttons that **deep-link straight to that work's page** (search-URL fallback if no deep link), (3) awareness content (personal risk + creator harm), (4) search/detail/Articles/Stay Safe, EN/KO i18n, responsive/accessible polish. Price as a light tag (free/wait-to-free/paid + last-checked date).
- **Data foundation** — first 80 titles: top 20 each from Naver Webtoon, KakaoPage, Ridi, Naver Series. Per title: **legal-platform deep-link URLs**, free-chapter range, ongoing events, light price tag. (Deep links are "one more data field," so absorbed into the collection work.)
- **Milestone (Jul 31):** web build complete + 80 titles (with deep links) loaded → launch-ready.


## Phase 2 — August: Validate & launch

**Goal:** prove quality with real users, then go public.

- **Data expansion** — grow the DB to ~160 titles; keep updating new titles, deep links, availability changes.
- **Beta test** — 15–20 testers (friends, acquaintances, school clubs). Test the full flow: search/discovery → routing to the legal site; a short Google Form rates accuracy and ease. Log errors (broken deep links, missing availability, search bugs).
- **Fix & polish** — high-priority feedback first; resolve all P1/P2 bugs.
- **Public launch** — launch; produce launch content (SNS card-news, infographics, announcement posts).
- **Milestone (end of Aug):** public launch live; availability/deep-link accuracy ≥95%; ≥90% of titles have a deep link; beta satisfaction ≥4.0.


## Phase 3 — September–October: Grow

**Goal:** convert the launch into users and awareness (organic-only) + spread the awareness message + start creator outreach early.

- **Events** (4):
  - Hidden-gem / favorite-title legal discovery challenge — post legally-read recommendations to SNS
  - Piracy risk/harm awareness quiz (personal risk + creator harm)
  - Legal-webtoon use proof/pledge event (payment/subscription screenshot + pledge)
  - Creator support-message campaign (delivered later in interviews)
- **Marketing channels (awareness-content led):**
  - Instagram — risk/harm-statistics card-news, discovery/how-to, event promo
  - YouTube / TikTok — piracy-risk shorts (malware, fraud, account theft), feature demos, recommendation curation
  - Blog / Naver Cafe — "why read legally" + "how to find legal sources" SEO posts
  - Communities (Everytime, webtoon gallery) — event + informational posts
  - Collaborations — copyright groups, creator associations, webtoon influencers
- **Awareness content** — publish 8+; internal-link to search/detail.
- **Early creator outreach** — leverage domestic-creator access to start contacting associations/creators before Phase 4.
- **Milestone (Oct 31):** 3,000 visitors, 1,000 SNS followers, 500 outbound deep-link clicks, 4 events, 5+ creators contacted.


## Phase 4 — November–December: Mission & close

**Goal:** measure and deliver attitude change, and close the year.

- **Attitude-change measurement** — pre/post survey on intent to use legal (measurement-honesty principle).
- **Revenue-structure / MG analysis** — research per-platform creator settlement ratios, revenue-share, and the MG system; analyze how legal use affects creator income; publish a comparison report.
- **Domestic creator interviews (video + shorts)** — prioritize domestic creators vocal on copyright/piracy; email associations, copyright bodies, rights groups; conduct 5 interviews (written or 20–30 min Zoom). **Publish interview videos in Fairtoon's "Creator Voices" section** and **edit key statements into shorts uploaded to YouTube / TikTok / Reels** (awareness reach + inflow at once). Compile key statements + harm cases.
- **Year-end wrap** — project completion report; reconcile final KPIs.
- **Milestone (Dec 31):** 5 interviews published, MG report published, attitude survey complete, project complete.


## Critical path & dependencies

- **Web build (Jul) gates everything** — beta, launch, and all marketing depend on a working site. Protect Jul 31; if at risk, cut scope (defer Articles depth, not the discovery/aggregator/awareness core).
- **Data + deep-link accuracy gates launch** — don't launch on broken deep links; the trustworthiness of legal routing is the product's core. (Not real-time — manage expectations with the "last-checked" date.)
- **Launch + traffic (Aug–Oct) gate interviews (Nov–Dec)** — creators respond more once there's an audience. But domestic creators are accessible, so outreach starts early in Phase 3.
- **Founder bandwidth is the main constraint** — single student, no budget → sequenced (build → launch → grow → mission); no overlapping phases.
