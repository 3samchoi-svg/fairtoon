## How to use these

- **Measurement honesty:** real-world "piracy reduction" is unmeasurable. Instead, the core impact proxies are (1) attitude-change survey, (2) outbound deep-link clicks (legal-conversion proxy), and (3) legal-use pledges/proof.
- **Leading vs lagging:** product/data (group 1) are leading; growth and mission (groups 2–3) are lagging and depend on launch quality.
- **Re-baseline after launch:** treat the Aug launch as the moment to replace estimates with real data and reset Sep–Dec targets.
- **No-budget reality:** growth numbers assume organic-only reach (SEO + communities + short-form). Revise upward if a paid/partnership channel opens.
- **Data-target reconciliation:** 80→160 operational; 150–300 year-end stretch.
