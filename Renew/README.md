## Strategy pivot — confirmed by the CEO on 2026-06-27 (5 decisions)

Following [`00_Strategy_Reevaluation`](./00_Strategy_Reevaluation.md) and an enforcement fact-check, the CEO made five decisions, now reflected in 01·02·03:

1. **Demote price comparison** — to a light auxiliary tag only (free / wait-to-free / paid + last-checked date).
2. **Redefine the mission** — from "reduce the (unmeasurable) use of illegal sites" to **"shift awareness/attitudes about illegal use + convert intent into legal use."**
3. **Korea first (v1) → international expansion (v2)** — for a student's extracurricular/portfolio + community contribution, executional credibility and authenticity come first.
4. **Elevate awareness to a core pillar** — legal can't beat "free," so the only lever that moves people is a change in awareness/attitude. Combine awareness (motivation) + utility (friction removal) as a **funnel**.
5. **Add a legal-site aggregator (deep links, level 2a)** — per title, jump straight to the work's page on legal platforms. Goes beyond an abstract awareness page to make legal use a single click. (Real-time tracking = level 3 is out of scope.)

> **Enforcement premise corrected:** the prior version's "Korea's enforcement is shrinking the problem" was factually wrong — in 2026, despite emergency blocking, Newtoki returned within a day and sites grew (whack-a-mole). Supply can't be killed, so the logic is replaced with **demand/awareness is the only durable lever** — which further justifies awareness-as-core + Korea-first.
