# 01 · Project Goals (2026 H2)


> **Revised 2026-06-27 (v2).** Reflects the CEO's five decisions — (1) demote price comparison, (2) redefine the mission, (3) Korea first, (4) **elevate awareness to a core pillar**, (5) **add a legal-site aggregator (deep links)**. Basis: [`00_Strategy_Reevaluation`](./00_Strategy_Reevaluation.md) + the 2026-06-27 enforcement fact-check.


## Vision

A world where reading webtoons legally is the easiest, most obvious choice — so that piracy loses its main advantage and creators are fairly rewarded.


## Mission (redefined)

**Shift awareness and attitudes** toward illegal webtoon use (why you shouldn't use illegal sites), and **convert that intent into action by making legal use a single click away** (where, what, and how).

- **What changed:** the old mission was "reduce the (unmeasurable) use of illegal sites." We retired the unmeasurable outcome but **restored awareness (attitude change) to the front as the campaign's core purpose**, and paired it with a utility that turns that attitude into behavior.
- **Dual framing:** self-interest (**personal risk** — malware, payment fraud, account theft) + creator harm (values alignment). Pure moral lecturing is the weakest form of awareness and is avoided.


## Target audience (agreed with Jiho, 2026-06-28)

- **Primary target:** people in Korea who **read pirated webtoons** — **mainly students**. (We target the people who need to change, not those who already read legally.)
- **Content scope:** **Korean webtoons** (v1 data scope).
- Because the target is students, tune tone/channels (short-form, communities, SNS) and the awareness message (personal risk) accordingly.


## The core model: awareness and utility as a funnel

Legal can never beat **free** on price or convenience. So the only lever that moves someone from illegal to legal is a change in values/awareness. But awareness alone doesn't produce behavior because of the intention-action gap. We combine the two as a funnel:

- **Awareness = motivation (why)** — builds the willingness to "choose legal even at some inconvenience." **This is the campaign's true purpose.**
- **Utility = friction removal (how)** — minimizes the inconvenience so that willingness doesn't die in the gap.

> **Why Korea-first + awareness-as-core (enforcement premise corrected):** in 2026, despite Korea's emergency-blocking system, Newtoki returned within a day and the number of sites actually grew (whack-a-mole). **Supply (the sites) cannot be killed.** So the only structurally durable intervention is **demand-side = why people use them (awareness)**. The fact that enforcement fails actually justifies an awareness-centered approach.


## The site's core features (3+1)

1. **Discovery** — find/recommend what to read (Korean-webtoon curation). *Utility*
2. **Legal-site aggregator** — per title, **deep links that jump straight to that work's page** on legal platforms (Naver Webtoon, KakaoPage, Ridi, Naver Series, etc.). Routes scattered legal paths from one place. *Utility*
3. **Awareness content** — why not to use illegal sites (personal risk + creator harm), enforcement/harm data, creator voices. *Motivation*
- **(+) Price as a light auxiliary tag** only (free / wait-to-free / paid + last-checked date). Real-time price / latest-chapter tracking is out of scope.

Rough weighting: discovery + aggregator 45 / awareness 35 / creator voices 20.


## The 4 strategic objectives

Each objective maps to a phase in the [Master Timeline](./03_Master_Timeline.md) and a KPI group in [02 · KPIs](./02_KPIs.md).


### Objective 1 — Ship the product (by end of July 2026)

Complete production of the FairToon website with **discovery + legal aggregator (deep links) + awareness** as its core, built on real data. Price as a light tag only. Move from "preview" to a launch-ready, production site.


### Objective 2 — Earn trust through quality data + a clean launch (August)

Build an accurate, current DB of legal-platform availability, deep links, and metadata; validate with real beta users; fix what matters; launch publicly with confidence.


### Objective 3 — Grow awareness and users (September–October)

Use the launch as a hook to grow new users and awareness through events and multi-channel content marketing, with awareness content as the central message. Leverage the Korea-first strength (access to domestic creators) to **begin creator relationship-building early in this phase**.


### Objective 4 — Deliver the core mission: creator voices + awareness (November–December)

Convert traffic and credibility into the mission — domestic creator interviews, a webtoon revenue-structure / MG analysis report, and a published "Creator Voices" body of work — then close out the year with a project completion report.


## Phasing: Korea (v1) → International (v2)

- **v1 (2026 H2):** Korean webtoons only. All four objectives working, validated, and completed in the Korean market.
- **v2 (2027+ / post-launch):** building on v1's working product + creator relationships, **expand into an international/English-language legal-availability aggregator** — the "scaling impact" narrative for the portfolio/admissions angle.


## Scope guardrails

- **Awareness messaging is up-front across the site.** But keep Home/search/discovery's "find" experience a clean utility, placing persuasion around title cards / dedicated sections / content so it doesn't block the utility flow.
- Differentiate through the **combination** of a Korean legal aggregator + discovery + awareness + domestic creator voices. The aggregator alone isn't novel; the combination is.
- Keep the established design language (light theme, Archivo Black + Inter, blue `#2F5FEF` accent, pill nav/buttons, rounded cards).
- Bilingual EN / KO (UI). Data scope for v1 = Korean titles.


## Out of scope for 2026 H2 (parked)

- **Real-time price / free-status / latest-chapter tracking (aggregator level 3)** — highest maintenance cost (no official API, policies change without notice). Light tag (last-checked date) only.
- Depth on international/English-language data → phased to v2.
- Platform affiliate/referral monetization — noted as a possibility, not pursued in v1.
- Campaign-era features held for later: Electronic Signature, Report a Pirate, interactive revenue calculator, dedicated Data Lab.
- Japanese language. The `Explore` page (parked pending separate decision).
